TARGET AND CONDITIONAL JUDGMENT
Target 4730/4920: for every totally real algebraic integer alpha, an actual unitary
group-theoretical category and ONE self-dual SIMPLE X with m alpha in the REGULAR fusion
spectrum, m a controlled positive integer, with explicit bounds.

No published theorem covering this entire endpoint was located in the bounded searches below.
This is not an exhaustive novelty certificate. A proof of that target would resolve the precise
published LPR Question 2.12 negatively, even in the integral unitary group-theoretical class,
and considerably strengthen a lone example. The meaningful consequence concerns individual
fusion-matrix eigenvalues. It does not establish noncyclotomic fields of definition, a failure
of cyclotomic modular data, a new graph theorem, or exact equality of an entire spectral field
with a prescribed number field. Scaling leaves the field of the selected eigenvalue unchanged.

The strongest adverse significance case is genuine: most of the ingredients, including the
forgetful orbit-sum computation, are old. A short reassembly without the full evaluated
universal consequence/bounds would be insufficient for this journal. Conversely, short proof
length alone would not nullify a genuinely new uniform answer to an explicit open question.
Final significance depends on what the COMPLETE account actually proves and how directly the
new consequence is already implicit in the closest existing categorical results. No favorable
whole correctness or publication verdict is issued here.
